### Signature effects

The set pieces that make the page 6labs: the tile floor and its states, the holograms, the glyph field, the scroll line, the accent water and the families of halftone, fringe and grain. Each chapter says what the effect is, how it is drawn and how to change it safely. Layer stack (5.1) opens the group because every effect sits on one of its planes, and the Compositor-safe rule (5.13) closes it because every effect keeps it.
